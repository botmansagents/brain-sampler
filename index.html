What the brain does

  • Finds the owner on the county appraisal record.
  • Reads the county clerk index for deeds, loans, releases and liens tied to the property.
  • Checks the county tax record for taxes due.
  • Puts it in plain words so you can see the chain of owners and anything still open.

All of these records are public and free. This sampler never charges you anything.
